# Value Proposition

We help growing companies transform Odoo from a traditional ERP into an AI-powered business operating system. Instead of depending on consultants or agencies for every change, the client's team operates the system while Claude AI handles operational complexity — reducing manual work, accelerating decision-making, and enabling scalable growth.

## This solves

- Operations have become a bottleneck to growth
- Spreadsheets, manual work, or an expensive ERP partner create delays, high cost, and low trust in the system
- Leaders lack operational visibility and cannot evolve the business fast enough because every change depends on external support

## Main use cases

- Odoo ERP implementation and business process standardization
- Reducing dependency on agencies by enabling internal teams to operate and evolve Odoo
- Embedding Claude AI inside Odoo to automate workflows, reporting, and day-to-day operations

| **Company size sub-band** | **Contacts to pursue** | **Direction**                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| 40–100 employees          | 2–3 contacts           | Top-down — Founder/CEO or COO/VP Operations first, then layer in CFO or CIO/IT Director once the primary contact engages or stalls                                              |
| 100–250 employees         | 3–4 contacts           | Top-down, wider spread — Founder/CEO or COO in parallel with CFO and CIO/IT Director; more decision layers at this size justify reaching more of the priority-star list at once |

*Direction stays top-down because the priority-star table already ranks Founder/CEO and COO/VP Operations highest — a bottom-up entry (e.g. Operations Manager) adds a hop without adding leverage. Sequence personas from this table once persona-driven branching is in play, rather than contacting every persona simultaneously.*

# 5. Fit Score Rubric — Used by the Evaluation Tool

*Purpose: this is what narrows the wide pool from Section 1 down to real prospects. It runs after sourcing, not before — so none of this should be used to exclude a profile from the search itself.*

## Company-level

- 10–250 employees
- Operates in Wholesale, Distribution, Import/Export, E-commerce, Light Manufacturing, Consumer Goods, Retail, Logistics/3PL, Construction/Trades, Field Services, Food & Beverage, Professional Services, or Franchise/multi-unit
- Experiencing growth that is creating operational complexity
- Relies on spreadsheets, manual processes, or disconnected systems, OR already has an ERP that is slow/expensive to modify
- Has visible operational bottlenecks affecting speed, visibility, or scalability
- Shows frustration (public or inferred) with the cost, speed, or trustworthiness of current systems or implementation partner
- Has hired or is hiring Operations Managers, ERP Administrators, Business Systems, or Systems Coordinators
- Shows a recent growth trigger — hiring, expansion, or new business initiatives
- Is looking to improve operational efficiency, not simply purchase new software
- Has the budget and executive willingness to invest if ROI is clear
- Values speed, visibility, and operational control over maintaining the status quo
- Is not highly bureaucratic or resistant to adopting modern business systems

## Person-level

- An active executive or operational leader — not a board member, investor, or external advisor
- Holds one of: Founder, Co-Founder, CEO, COO, VP Operations, Operations Director, Operations Manager, Head of Operations, CFO/Finance Director, CIO/IT Director, Commercial Director/VP Sales
- Owns or strongly influences operational performance and business systems
- Directly responsible for improving efficiency, scalability, or operational visibility
- Experiences the operational bottlenecks firsthand
- Has influence over ERP or business-systems decisions
- Is actively seeking to reduce manual work and dependency on external consultants
- Is measured on operational efficiency, process execution, cost reduction, or growth
- Would likely sponsor a discovery call if it uncovers measurable operational improvement
- Is frustrated by slow system changes, poor visibility, or inefficiency and is motivated to solve it

# 6. Outreach Playbook

## Discovery split

Track A — Pre-System: spreadsheets/manual work. Track B — Stuck with a System: existing ERP that is slow/expensive to change. Same sourcing and search strategy for both; the split happens on the discovery call via one qualifying question:
***“Is your current setup mostly spreadsheets and manual work, or do you already have a system and just find changes to it slow and expensive?”***

## Primary CTA

Free 30-minute Discovery Call. Every message, regardless of persona or track, ends with this single ask — not a demo, not a proposal, not a pitch.

- Review their current setup (spreadsheets, existing system, or agency relationship)
- Identify where the biggest cost or bottleneck actually is
- Show them what the first 30 days would look like if they moved forward
